How a day is called: the 12:49 PT close is the anchor. The first bar from 12:50 to 13:15 PT that moves 5 points from it is the setup, faded back toward it. Target 2 points from the anchor; price stop 12 points from it; time stop 7 minutes after the excursion. Stops are checked before the target, from the bar after the excursion. The indicator does not price fills: points here assume a fill at the trigger level, or at the excursion bar's close in the "close fill" column. "Chart check" compares these calls with the markers the chart itself printed. VIX skip is the framework rule, shown for context; it changes nothing here. Fewer than 30 setups is a description, not evidence.
