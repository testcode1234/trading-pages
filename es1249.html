How a day is called: the 12:49 PT close is the anchor. The first bar from 12:50 to 13:15 PT that moves 5 points from it is the setup, faded back toward it. Target 2 points from the anchor; price stop 12 points from it; time stop 7 minutes after the excursion. Stops are checked before the target, from the bar after the excursion. The indicator does not price fills: points here assume a fill at the trigger level, or at the excursion bar's close in the "close fill" column. Dollars are $50 a point for one ES contract, before commissions and slippage. "Chart check" compares these calls with the markers the chart itself printed. Days marked "Yahoo backfill" predate the recorder: they are re-derived from Yahoo 1-minute bars, which match the chart's highs and lows but can differ by a quarter point on a close, and have no chart markers to check against. VIX skip is the framework rule, shown for context; it changes nothing here. Fewer than 30 setups is a description, not evidence.
